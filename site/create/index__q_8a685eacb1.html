Fold Fractal

Fold a glowing strip in half again and again and open every crease to the same angle: each fold copies the whole line and swings the copy about its end, so the layers double until a dragon curve appears. Start from a strip, a T, a wave or a lightning bolt, fold at 90, 108 or 120 degrees, left or right, up to 26 times, then let two or four dragons meet at one corner, fill the screen, or unfold it all again. Every fold plays the next note of a rising scale.
