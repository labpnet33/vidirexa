Physics simulation video maker

Physics simulations are satisfying because nothing is animated by hand: blocks land crooked and wobble, balls ricochet and multiply, a 3D race is decided on the track. You set the rules, and the simulation decides the rest.

Each game here runs its simulation in your browser. Change the settings, preview the whole run for free and record it as a vertical video.

How to make a physics simulation video

Open a game in the studio, change its settings, press preview to watch the full simulation, then sign in and record. The video downloads as a vertical 9:16 MP4 for TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts.

Change one setting at a time and preview again: a small change makes a different run.

Which simulation to pick

Wobble Stack drops soft blocks into a 3D cabinet with soft-body physics before a press squashes the pile. Egg Simulation has five egg physics studies with fifteen layouts. Shape Merger and Fruit Merge have tunable physics, Perfect Swish bounces waves of balls off a curved floor into a hoop, and Marble Race is a seeded 3D race.

Questions

Is the motion simulated or animated?

Simulated. The motion comes from each game’s physics and your settings, so different settings give a different result. Seeded games such as Marble Race replay the same run for the same seed and settings.

Which physics settings can I change?

It depends on the game, for example bounciness, friction, speed or materials. Each game page lists exactly what you can customize.

Is it free?

Preview is free without an account. The Free plan records all free games with a watermark; each week some games need Creator or Pro and carry a badge.
