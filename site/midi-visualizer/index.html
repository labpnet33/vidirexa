MUSIC & MIDI VIDEOS

MIDI visualizer online

A good music visualization makes every note visible: a ball lands on a pad, a key lights up, a corner chimes. These games generate the level from the tune, so the motion follows the music exactly.

Load your own MIDI file in Melody Bounce or Roll Hopper, or pick one of the built-in public-domain classics and original songs, then record a vertical video.

How to turn a MIDI file into a video

Open Melody Bounce or Roll Hopper, choose the song selector and load your MIDI file (or pick a built-in tune). Set the tempo, instrument and look, press preview and check that the motion matches the music, then sign in and record. The video downloads as a vertical 9:16 MP4.

Use only music you have the rights to. The built-in classics are public-domain compositions played by the studio’s own instruments.

Which visualizer to pick

Melody Bounce: a ball bounces from pad to pad, one note per hit. Roll Hopper: a ball hops across a glowing piano roll and the keys light up. Musical Path: a ball travels a flowing course on the beat, with your own song. Chime Chamber builds polyrhythms from nested polygons, Pulse Bloom plays a generated melody on every bounce, and Sort Spectrum turns sorting algorithms into sound.

Questions

Can I upload my own MIDI file?

Yes, in Melody Bounce and Roll Hopper. Musical Path accepts an uploaded song instead.

Is the MIDI visualizer free?

You can preview without an account and record all free games on the Free plan with a watermark. Each week some games need Creator or Pro; they carry a badge.

Does the ball really hit the notes on time?

Yes. The level is generated from the tune, so each hit is the next note at its exact time.
