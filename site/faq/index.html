What is Vidirexa?

Vidirexa (vidirexa.com) is a browser studio for satisfying simulation videos. Pick a game such as Marble Race, customize it, preview it free and record a vertical 9:16 MP4 for TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts.

Seen the vidirexa.com watermark on a video?

That video was recorded here. You can make your own version of it: open Create, pick the same game, change its settings and record. Free recordings carry the vidirexa.com watermark; Creator, Pro and Lifetime recordings have none.

Is Vidirexa free?

Yes. Free includes all free games and 2 successful recordings a day with a watermark, no editing skills or software needed. Paid plans start at $4.99/month without the watermark.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser, on desktop or phone. You can preview every game without an account; sign in to record.

How do I make a video?

Open a game in the studio, change its settings, press preview to watch the full simulation, then sign in and record. The video downloads as a vertical 9:16 MP4 for TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts.

Can I use my own music or photos?

Many games let you pick a built-in public-domain song or load your own MIDI file, and some accept your own photos, which stay on your device. Only use music and pictures you have the rights to use.

Which games are free this week?

Which games are free changes every week: about 15% are PRO (Pro or Lifetime) and 30% are CREATOR (Creator, Pro or Lifetime), marked with a badge; the 10 most-made games and featured games are always free. Preview and customize are always free, and a recording you already started is honoured.

A few good questions.

Can I try it for free?

Yes. The Free plan includes all free games, basic customizations, and 2 successful recordings each day with a Vidirexa watermark. Sign in before recording.

Which games need a paid plan?

Which games are free changes every week: about 15% are PRO (Pro or Lifetime) and 30% are CREATOR (Creator, Pro or Lifetime), marked with a badge; the 10 most-made games and featured games are always free. Preview and customize are always free, and a recording you already started is honoured. Free records all free games, Creator records free and CREATOR games, Pro and Lifetime record all games.

What video format do I get?

Recordings download as vertical 9:16 MP4 video, the portrait format used by TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Free recordings carry a Vidirexa watermark; paid plans have none.

How do daily limits work?

Free includes 2 and Creator 15 successful recordings per day. Pro and Lifetime have no daily recording count limit, subject to fair-use rate and concurrency limits. Allowances reset at 00:00 UTC and only successful recordings count. On Free, each free game also has a shared number of free videos per 24 hours for all Free accounts, which can be full (each slot frees up 24 hours after its video was made); your first video is always allowed, and Creator, Pro and Lifetime have no shared limit.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel a monthly plan from Manage subscription in your account, or contact us for help. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your paid billing period; you keep access until then and will not be charged again.

Do you offer refunds?

Except where required by law, subscription payments are non-refundable and there are no partial or prorated refunds for unused time. Lifetime purchases are non-refundable once used. The Terms & refunds page explains the details and your statutory rights.

How do ads work?

Free includes site ads and in-game ads where supported. A rewarded ad may be offered before download when a supported provider is connected. Every paid plan removes ads everywhere. Ads are never burned into exported videos.

What does Lifetime include?

Lifetime includes Pro benefits forever for one $79.99 payment. Existing Lifetime purchases keep their price and benefits even if pricing changes later.

How do I start a paid plan?

Sign in, choose a plan and you are taken to secure Stripe checkout. If checkout is unavailable, Vidirexa tells you and no payment is taken.

Can I pay once instead of subscribing?

Yes. A Day pass (24 hours) or Week pass (7 days) gives every Pro benefit for one payment and never renews. Free users can also remove the watermark with a single small payment: 1 purchase = 2 watermark-free videos. Prices depend on your region and start at $0.59; the price shown on /pricing is the price charged.

Is there a free trial?

New accounts may be offered a 3-day Pro trial with a card. You pay $0 today; Pro then renews monthly at the price shown unless you cancel or switch to Creator before the trial ends. We email a reminder about 1 day before. One trial per person.
