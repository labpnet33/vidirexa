YOUR DATA, EXPLAINED

Privacy notice.

How Vidirexa handles your account, recordings and optional preferences.

Updated 18 November 2026

At a glance: we use account information to provide sign-in and keep the service secure. Optional website analytics and optional marketing choices are off unless you opt in. Account holders receive onboarding and offer emails from Vidirexa and can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email. When you purchase a paid plan on the website, Stripe processes your payment; in our mobile apps, Apple or Google does. We keep billing and consent records. DRAFT, pending owner review: the website may show banners outside the creation pages and an optional rewarded video for Free users; paid plans see no ads. the mobile apps show ads only on the Free plan. Available games support previewing, recording and private video downloads.

You can browse without creating an account. Cookies, storage and retention

Daily audience pseudonyms and their random secret are deleted from the active measurement tables after the UTC day, on the next arrival or hourly cleanup (normally within one hour). Daily aggregate totals are kept for 730 days. Temporary pseudonyms are pseudonymous data, not a claim of irreversible anonymity; infrastructure backups follow the provider retention described below.

The essential vireel_session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Signing out removes that session. Google sign-in uses a temporary vireel_oauth cookie for up to 10 minutes. Sign-in inside our mobile app, and the Google One Tap prompt, use a single-use server code for up to 10 minutes instead. When the One Tap prompt is offered, Google's script may set its own g_state cookie on our site to remember whether you closed it, and your browser keeps vx_onetap_dismissed and vx_onetap_ambient in session storage (cleared when the browser session ends) and a vx_onetap_dismissed_at time in local storage, so we respect your choice; none contains your email or account. These are separate from optional analytics.

For abuse prevention (see above), the first-party cookies vdx_bid (a random identifier) and vdx_fp (the fingerprint digest) last up to 400 days. Copies are kept in local storage (vdx_bid, and vdx_fp with the time it was calculated, recalculated after 7 days) and in IndexedDB (vdx), so that clearing one copy does not create a new identifier. They contain no email or browsing history, are not used for analytics or advertising and are not shared with anyone. Your browser sends them with requests to our site, but our server keeps their keyed hashes only for the account events described above.

If you allow analytics, vireel_analytics holds a browser consent token for up to 30 days. A local-storage timestamp, vireel-analytics-updated, synchronises preference changes between tabs; it does not contain your email or browsing history. That timestamp has no automatic expiry. Clearing browser storage removes it.

When you arrive through a referral link, local storage key vireel_ref holds the referral code and arrival time, in your browser only, for 30 days; a newer referral link replaces it. When you arrive through a link with campaign labels, vireel_first_touch holds those labels, the landing page path and arrival time for 30 days and is not replaced by later links. Both are sent to us only when you create an account, are deleted from your browser after a successful signup or when the 30 days end, and clearing the referral code field on the signup form deletes vireel_ref. Campaign labels saved with an account remain while the account is held.

So that an interrupted video is not lost, the studio keeps the recording settings of a video in progress in your browser's IndexedDB storage, and the finished MP4 file while it is being uploaded. This stays on your device only. It is deleted when the video is saved, when you cancel it, after a recording error, or after 7 days at most. If your browser runs out of storage, only the settings are kept. A video you finish without an account is kept the same way, in a separate IndexedDB store on your device, with its settings and random identifier, for up to 30 days, so that you can still sign up and download it on a later visit (we then show a "Your video is waiting" reminder at most once per browser session). It is deleted when you save it to an account, when you discard or delete it, after 30 days, and beyond the newest 3 such videos. Nothing of it is sent to us until you save it. Your browser may remove it sooner (for example in private windows, or Safari after 7 days without a visit). On our server, the settings of each recording are kept for 30 days so the "Restart video" notification can reopen the same video.

  • Optional browsing events: cleaned up after 30 days, or when the consent period expires. Revoking in Privacy preferences deletes events linked to this browser’s current consent immediately from the active database. It does not identify other browsers or erase backup copies.
  • Aggregate arrivals: daily source counts remain for up to 730 days. An authorised operator can set a random vireel_internal_analytics cookie for up to 365 days to exclude that browser, including after sign-out. Only its hash and expiry are held; it is not used as a visitor identifier or linked to an account. Verified internal accounts are excluded automatically and receive this exclusion cookie.
  • Essential account-event details: 90 days. A deduplicated account ID and verified signup date are retained for up to 730 days to count new accounts accurately; recognised internal IDs remain excluded while held. Recording-event details also have a 90-day window; older recording events roll into daily counts by game and plan without account IDs for up to 730 days. Hashed event identifiers used to prevent duplicate counting also have a 730-day window. Analytics cleanup runs when the service starts and every hour, so deletion follows the next run. Recording counts use UTC calendar days.
  • Authentication: signup/reset codes and Google flow state expire after 10 minutes; rate-limit entries after 15 minutes. Expiry prevents use, but expired database records are removed when a later request triggers cleanup, rather than on a guaranteed deletion schedule. Expired session records also have no scheduled deletion.
  • Account data: email, credentials and account/plan records remain while your account is held. There is currently no automatic inactivity deletion. You can delete your account yourself under Account, Delete account (website and mobile app), or contact us to request erasure; signing out does not delete an account. Deleting an account removes its sign-in, profile, saved videos and their files, notifications, email preferences and pending emails at once; payment, tax, fraud-prevention and pseudonymous measurement records that we must or may keep remain linked only to an anonymised account record, and the email address is added to our hashed suppression list so we send it no further marketing. Completed recording files are available privately to their owner for 30 days after successful completion.
