FAQ

Questions partners ask.

Is my partner code the same as my referral code?

Yes. You have one 4-character code, shown on your Referrals page together with your link (vidirexa.com/account?ref=YOURCODE). The same code tracks the partner commission and the small $1 referral credit, and both can apply to the same referral.

What do my followers get?

When someone creates their Vidirexa account with your code (through your link or by typing it at signup), they get 10 bonus recordings right away and 30% off their first month of a monthly Creator or Pro plan. The code has to be entered when the account is created and cannot be added later. Lifetime is not discounted.

What if someone clicks my link but signs up later?

Your link is remembered for 30 days in the browser it was opened in, on any Vidirexa page, and your code is filled in automatically when they create their account there. It still only counts if the account is created with your code: if they later open someone else’s link, the most recent link is used, and a different browser or device, cleared browser storage or a removed code means it is not applied. Typing your code at signup always works.

How much do I earn?

50% of every payment a referred user actually makes (after discounts, excluding tax), for up to 12 months from that user's first payment. It covers every qualifying payment in that window, renewals included, and stops if they cancel. Pro is $9.99/month, so each Pro subscriber earns you about $5 a month. The first 100 approved partners lock in the 50% rate.

When does a commission become payable?

Each commission is pending for 30 days after the payment, then becomes payable. Refunded or charged-back payments do not count; if a refund happens after you were paid, it is deducted from your next payout.

How and when are payouts sent?

Monthly, by PayPal, once your payable balance reaches $20. For now we send payouts by hand. When you first reach $20 we email you to ask for your PayPal email address, the only detail we need. Automatic payouts on the site are planned.

Where can I see my referrals and earnings?

Approved partners see their pending, payable and paid balances and their number of referrals on this Get Paid page. Your code, link and promo credit are on your Referrals page.

What is the $15 Pro credit and how do I use it?

Approved partners get $15 of Vidirexa credit. It has no cash value and works on monthly Creator or Pro plans. Choose Pro on the Pricing page and tick "Use up to ... referral credit": the first month ($9.99) is covered and the rest stays for a later month. Stripe asks for a card at checkout and the plan renews monthly until you cancel; you can cancel any time before the renewal.

My credit shows an amount as "reserved". What does that mean?

You started a checkout with credit and did not finish it. The amount is held for that checkout. It is used if you complete the checkout, or released automatically when the checkout expires (within 24 hours).

Can I share on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and X?

Yes. Use the same code and link on every platform, in any language. You do not need to register your other accounts with us.

May I post and monetize videos made with Vidirexa?

Yes. The game visuals and the built-in game sound are made by Vidirexa, and you may post and monetize your videos on any platform. If you add your own music, images or footage, you need the rights to them, and you must follow each platform’s rules. Free-plan videos carry a small Vidirexa watermark; Creator, Pro and Lifetime videos do not.

How should I disclose the partnership?

Mark posts with your platform’s paid-partnership or branded-content label where it is required, or add #ad. For example: "Made with Vidirexa. Use code YOURCODE at vidirexa.com for 10 bonus recordings and 30% off your first month #ad".

Partners earn 50% of each payment actually made (after discounts, excluding tax) by users who sign up with their code, for up to 12 months from each user's first payment. Commission is earned only on payments made; it stops if the user cancels. Commissions become payable 30 days after each payment; refunded or charged-back payments don't count. Minimum payout $20, paid monthly. Label posts as an ad or partnership where your platform requires it.
